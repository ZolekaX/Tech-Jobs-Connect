Career Tips

CV Writing

Keep your CV clear and professional. Highlight your technical skills, education, experience and projects.

Interview Preparation

Research the company, practise common interview questions and prepare examples of your technical skills.

Skills Development

Keep learning new technologies and practise your skills by working on personal and academic projects.

Starting a Career in IT

Build a strong portfolio, gain practical experience and apply for internships, graduate programmes and entry-level technology positions.
