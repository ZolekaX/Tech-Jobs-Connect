Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register as a job seeker?

Select the Job Seeker Registration option and complete the registration form.

How do I apply for a job?

Open the job listing, read the job requirements and click the Apply Now button.

Can I upload my CV?

Yes. Job seekers can upload their CV when creating their profile.

How can a company advertise a vacancy?

Companies must register as employers before they can advertise available positions.

Is registration free?

Yes. Registration on Tech Jobs Connect is free for job seekers and employers.

Can I apply for more than one job?

Yes. Registered job seekers can apply for multiple suitable vacancies.
